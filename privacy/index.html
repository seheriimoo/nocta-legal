Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18 · Product: Nocta

Nocta (“we”, “us”) is operated by Seher Akbulut, an individual developer. Nocta provides a nighttime sleep-transition experience.

1. Information you provide

You may type messages during a night session. Those messages are processed to generate a reply and guide the sleep transition. Nocta V1 does not require creating an account or providing your name or email to use the core night path.

2. AI conversation content

The chat library on this device saves the conversation you see: your messages and Nocta's replies. That library does not contain the prompt prepared for the model.

To write a reply, the app prepares a prompt from your current message and recent conversation. That prepared prompt is sent over the internet to Nocta's server relay, which forwards it to a third-party language-model provider (OpenAI). The prepared prompt is not saved in the chat library. Do not share information you are not comfortable sending to a third-party AI service.

3. Inferred information

On device, Nocta may derive temporary session signals (for example emotional/mental-pattern labels). A limited subset of pattern identifiers and related metadata may be stored locally for cross-night continuity. Dialogue transcripts are not stored in that continuity store. They are stored in the on-device chat library described above.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you subscribe or buy premium access, purchase status is handled through Apple and our payments partner (RevenueCat). We do not store your full payment card details in the app.

5. Crash reports

If crash reporting is enabled for a build, technical crash diagnostics may be sent to Sentry. The app is configured to avoid sending payment secrets and to scrub likely conversation payloads where feasible.

6. Device storage

The app stores local preferences, continuity data, and the chat library on your device (consent flags, session counters, last sleep-blocker hint, pattern metadata, and saved conversations). You can delete a saved chat in the app. Uninstalling the app typically removes this on-device data.

7. Not for medical or crisis use

Nocta is not a medical service and is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, therapy, or emergency support. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline.

8. How we use information

We use this information to operate the night conversation, generate replies, unlock premium session length when purchased, keep basic local continuity, and maintain app stability.

9. Third parties

  • Nocta's server relay. It forwards the prepared prompt to OpenAI. Its own stored records are the App Attest public key and signature counter for this install, request and spend counters used to enforce limits, and a verification challenge. Those records do not include your chat transcript. The challenge record is deleted when it is used. This policy states no retention period for an unused challenge or for the other relay records.
  • OpenAI — generates the cloud reply
  • Apple — App Store / In-App Purchase
  • RevenueCat — subscription entitlement status
  • Sentry — crash diagnostics (when configured)

OpenAI, Apple, RevenueCat, and Sentry, when used, retain data under their own policies. Those policies are not Nocta's, and this policy does not restate them.

10. Retention

Saved chats remain on the device until you delete them in the app or uninstall it. Other on-device consent and continuity data remain until you uninstall the app. In-app deletion of Living Mind continuity data is not yet available. Nocta's relay records are separate from the chat library. This policy sets no retention period for them.

11. Your choices

You may decline consent screens and not use cloud AI chat features. You may delete an individual saved chat in the app. Manage Apple subscriptions in Apple ID settings. For privacy requests, email nocta.app.support@proton.me. In-app Living Mind deletion UI is not yet shipped in V1; uninstalling the app typically clears local app storage on your device.

12. Age requirement and children

Nocta is intended only for users aged 18 and older. Nocta is not directed to children or minors and is not a children’s app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If we become aware that personal information has been provided by a person under 18, contact nocta.app.support@proton.me. We will take reasonable steps available to us, which may include deleting on-device continuity data associated with that use where we can identify it and guiding you to uninstall the app to clear local storage. We do not claim technical capabilities beyond what the current product supports (for example, there is no in-app Living Mind deletion control in V1, and third-party cloud providers retain data under their own policies).

This contractual minimum age (18+) is separate from Apple’s App Store age rating, which is configured in App Store Connect.

13. International processing

Cloud providers may process data in the United States or other countries where they operate.

14. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
